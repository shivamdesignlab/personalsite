Persona Driven Collaborative Storytelling (PDCS): Community Research

June 2023 – August 2024

Overview

Persona Driven Collaborative Storytelling (PDCS) is an innovative community-based research methodology that combines persona creation with collaborative narrative building to surface lived experiences of marginalized communities. This project details the full lifecycle of PDCS — from conceptual design through field deployment — presenting two rich case studies conducted with Indian international students navigating life in the United States. Through these studies, we demonstrate how PDCS enables participants to externalize personal experiences through fictional personas, creating a psychologically safe space for sharing sensitive topics such as cultural adjustment, identity negotiation, and systemic barriers.

Research Objectives

  1. Design and validate the PDCS methodology for community research
  2. Conduct case studies with Indian international student communities
  3. Evaluate PDCS effectiveness compared to traditional interview methods
  4. Develop a toolkit for researchers to adopt PDCS in their own work

Methodology

Design-based research with iterative methodology development, two longitudinal case studies with 24 participants, thematic analysis of generated narratives, and comparative evaluation with traditional focus group methods.
