Reconceptualizing Human–AI Experience from a Metaphysical Perspective

March 2025 – Present

Overview

This theoretical research project seeks to reframe our understanding of human–AI interaction through the lens of metaphysical philosophy. By drawing on ontological, epistemological, and phenomenological frameworks, we are constructing a comprehensive taxonomy that categorizes different modes of human–AI experience. This work challenges the predominantly utilitarian framing of AI interaction and proposes a richer vocabulary for understanding how humans relate to, perceive, and experience AI systems in their daily lives.

Research Objectives

  1. Develop a metaphysical framework for analyzing human–AI experiences
  2. Create a taxonomy of human–AI interaction modes based on philosophical traditions
  3. Conduct phenomenological studies of human–AI encounters
  4. Publish a position paper reframing HCAI research through metaphysical inquiry

Methodology

Philosophical analysis combined with phenomenological interviews, conceptual modeling, and interdisciplinary literature synthesis across philosophy, AI, and HCI.
