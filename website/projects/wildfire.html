How Discourse in Serious Games Shapes Collaborative Learning and Wildfire Preparedness

September 2024 – Present

Overview

This research investigates how serious games can improve wildfire preparedness by encouraging collaborative learning and community resilience. By analyzing the game FireSafe Friends, we utilized a mixed-methods approach that combined player surveys with a specialized discourse analysis framework. The study focuses on how in-game dialogue serves as a primary tool for players to co-construct knowledge, negotiate strategies, and manage resources under pressure.

Research Objectives

  1. Analyze discourse patterns in collaborative serious games for wildfire preparedness
  2. Identify key communication strategies that facilitate knowledge transfer
  3. Develop a methodology for evaluating collaborative learning in game-based disaster education
  4. Create design guidelines for effective serious games in emergency preparedness

Methodology

We employ a mixed-methods approach combining discourse analysis, observational analysis, and pre/post knowledge assessments. Game sessions were recorded and transcribed for detailed qualitative analysis.
