Psychological Well-being

January 2026 – Present

Overview

This project explores the intersection of psychological well-being and interactive digital experiences through two complementary research directions. The first examines how massively multiplayer online games (MMOs), particularly Sky: Children of the Light, create environments that support mental well-being through prosocial mechanics and emotional expression. The second direction investigates how AI-enabled embodied and multimodal interaction paradigms can be designed to support mental health interventions, exploring the role of physical movement, gesture, and multi-sensory feedback in therapeutic contexts.
