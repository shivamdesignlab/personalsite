Playbook: Virtual Pilot for Space Mission Success

September 2022 – December 2023

Overview

Playbook is an AI-augmented decision support system designed to enhance contingency planning capabilities for space mission operations. Building upon NASA's existing Playbook scheduling tool, this project introduces an intelligent virtual pilot that assists mission planners and crew members in navigating complex scheduling constraints, resource limitations, and unexpected events during space missions. The system leverages AI techniques to simulate mission scenarios, propose alternative plans, and evaluate risk factors in real-time.

Research Objectives

  1. Develop an AI-driven contingency planning module for Playbook
  2. Design intuitive interfaces for mission planners to interact with AI recommendations
  3. Evaluate system effectiveness through simulated mission scenarios
  4. Address human factors challenges in human-AI collaborative planning

Methodology

User-centered design process with NASA mission planners, iterative prototyping, wizard-of-oz testing, and simulated mission evaluation studies. Combines qualitative feedback from domain experts with quantitative performance metrics.

Related Publications

Shukla, S. et al. (2023). AI-Augmented Contingency Planning for Space Missions. AAAI HAI Workshop.
