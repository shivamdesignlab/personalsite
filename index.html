Hi! I am Shivam.

My research explores how people think, feel, and learn through computational artifacts.

I am a Research Associate at the Game User Interaction and Intelligence (GUII) Lab, led by Prof. Magy Seif El-Nasr. My research interests sit at the intersection of human–computer interaction, artificial intelligence (HCAI), psychological well-being, and metacognition. I have 4+ years of experience in mixed-methods research and product design.

I am grateful for all the support I have received throughout my journey. If you would like to talk about research or are looking for any advice I can offer, feel free to schedule a chat with me.

research questions I am interested in

The questions I carry with me

  • How could Human-Centered design approaches shape the future of space missions?
  • What do patterns of social interaction in games reveal about player well-being?
  • How does AI-enabled embodied and multimodal interaction shape mental health?
  • How can metaphysics help us understand and evaluate the quality of human–AI experience?

My Research and Design Philosophy

I believe that technology should be built with people, not just for them. Co-creation, critical thinking, and care are the key components of my research and design process. I love questioning assumptions, centering lived experience, and making space for ambiguity and nuance — especially in spaces shaped by AI. I would love to discuss more about my work.
